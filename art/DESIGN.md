# Helldiver design spec (original model)

The source of truth is `concept/final_turnaround.png`. `concept/final_helmet.png` shows the helmet in more detail; where the two
disagree, the turnaround wins. Concept A was picked, with concept C's helmet recoloured charcoal and no backpack (Terr4, 2026-10-24).

## Helmet
- Rounded charcoal dome with a **yellow stripe down the centre** of the dome, front to back (turnaround).
- A brow ridge over one wide **glowing amber visor band** (emission). The lower edge of the visor steps down slightly at the centre.
- A **jaw/mouth guard** with **two vents on each side** (the angled slots beside the mouth plate in the turnaround's front view;
  Terr4's note).
- Side ear plates; a collar/gorget piece with yellow trim at its top edge.

## Body
- **Armour:** charcoal plates with thin Super Earth yellow trim lines:
  - a big chest plate with a small hazard-stripe tag below the collar;
  - an abdomen plate;
  - two rounded, bevelled shoulder pads with yellow edges and a small chevron tag;
  - forearm guards;
  - thigh plates with yellow outlines;
  - shin guards with hazard-stripe tags.
- **Undersuit:** light grey (arms, legs, waist).
- **Belt:** grey, with boxy pouches (two on each side of the buckle).
- **Gloves:** dark charcoal and chunky. **Knee pads:** grey and rounded. **Boots:** grey and chunky, with a darker sole.
- **No backpack.** The jump pack stays separate equipment, shown only when equipped.

## Cape
- Long, dark charcoal, hanging from under the shoulder pads to the back of the knees, with a thin yellow stripe along the hem.
- An emblem of our own, drawn from scratch (Phase 7).

## Style rules
See `STYLE_GUIDE.md`: flat colour blocks from a palette atlas, modelled bevels, smoothness 0, emission only for the visor, and a
budget of about 11,000 triangles for the body (20,000 for everything).
